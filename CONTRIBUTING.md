# Contributing

Two rules, and they are the whole point of this list:

## 1. No unverified prices

AI coding tools reprice often. Windsurf's Pro tier moved $15 -> $20 and GitHub
Copilot's Pro+ moved $20 -> $39, both in July 2026. If you add or change a
price, link the vendor's own pricing page in your pull request.

Prices here are re-verified monthly and every change is recorded with the date
we observed it at https://devtoolsreview.com/changes/

## 2. Say what a tool is bad at

An entry that only lists strengths is an advertisement. One honest sentence
that includes a real limitation is worth more than a paragraph of features.

## Adding a tool

- One line, alphabetical within its section
- Link the vendor, not a referral or tracking URL
- Include the real entry price
- Disclose any affiliation you have with the tool

## Corrections

Especially welcome. Open an issue if something is out of date — it gets fixed
in the next monthly re-verification, and the correction is logged publicly.
